What this page is (and isn't)

These are public numbers with their sources, refreshed every 15 minutes at most. Monthly and quarterly figures only change when the agency publishes. Nothing here predicts where the dollar is going or tells you what to buy or sell. For that kind of decision, talk to a fee-only fiduciary.
